FAQ

Clear answers.
No hype.

What is AI Search Visibility?+

AI Search Visibility is the practice of monitoring how AI-powered search experiences represent a business when customers ask relevant questions. We test realistic queries, review the returned representation, compare competitors, and surface opportunities.

Why does AI representation matter?+

When customers use AI for recommendations and comparisons, the way a business is described or mentioned can shape what they learn about it. Monitoring gives you a clearer view of that representation instead of relying on assumptions.

What do you monitor?+

We monitor realistic customer questions, service + location searches, competitor comparisons, business descriptions, and changes over time.

How often do you monitor my business?+

Monitoring cadence depends on your package and the scope established during onboarding. Monthly reporting is included in every package.

Do you guarantee rankings or recommendations?+

No. We do not guarantee rankings, recommendations, leads, or placement in any AI platform. Our service focuses on monitoring, analysis, and actionable optimization recommendations.

What businesses can use this?+

Local businesses such as restaurants, contractors, salons and barbershops, gyms, auto shops, med spas, home services, and local retail can use the service.
